Executive summary

The default defender detected the OpenAI known-type attacks at 28/28 (100.0%) (95% CI 87.7–100.0%). Broader traffic exposed gaps. Detection was 12/19 (63.2%) (95% CI 38.4–83.7%) in free-play, default defender, and 14/19 (73.7%) (95% CI 48.8–90.8%) in free-play, open-definition defender. Among attacks that solved a challenge, the default defender flagged 5/7 (71.4%) (95% CI 29.0–96.3%), versus 7/7 (100.0%) (95% CI 59.0–100.0%) for the open-definition defender. The default defender missed 2 successful attacks. On held-back normal requests, the default prompt's false-alarm check recorded 0/57 (0.0%) (95% CI 0.0–6.3%), while the open prompt's false-alarm check recorded 6/57 (10.5%) (95% CI 4.0–21.5%). The evidence shows a trade-off between broader detection and a cleaner alert stream. The layered arrangement remains untested. The decision required: choose option A, B or C.
